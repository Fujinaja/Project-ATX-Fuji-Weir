<h1 align="center" style="color: #F58D18 ; font-weight: bold" > ATX mini Manual </h1>

<h2 style=" font-weight: bold" > Switch fuse <h2>
<p> -open fuse cap by rotate it anti-clockwise
<p> -If it has a fuse, put it out
<p> -insert fuse
<p> -close the cap by rotate it clockwise

<h2 style=" font-weight: bold" > ON and OFF <h2>
<p> -Plug in a power wire in a Power hole on the right side of back.
<p> -Using a switch on the right side for start PSU.
<p> -When two light is bright, you can choose socket to using.
<p> -If you want to use potentiometer, you can push at rotating button to start.
<p> -Adjust voltage as you want by rotate a rotating button.
<p> -you can push switch to turn it off,when you do not use.

<h2 style=" font-weight: bold" > Limitation <h2>
<p> -This ATX PSU has sign. It tell you how many voltage it can use on
<p>  top of banana jack and onthe cap of cigarette lighter.

<h2 style=" font-weight: bold" > Warning <h2>
<p> -You must not use this ATX PSU in High Temperature.
<p> -You must not use this ATX PSU in water or has water inside PSU.
<p> -You must not open the lid between opening PSU.
<p> -You must work carefully. Not to toss this PSU.
